## Slide 1: Introduction & Core Architecture
### A Privacy-First, Air-Gapped Cyber Security Shield

*   **100% Data Sovereignty**: Operates entirely within your local enterprise networks. Scraped messages, credentials, and logs are analyzed locally. No external APIs or third-party servers are queried.
*   **Air-Gapped Telegram Scraper**: Employs Python's Telethon API to safely join and crawl channels, supporting secure OTP and Two-Factor (2FA) client registration processes.
*   **Cost-Efficient Offline Operations**: Runs local intelligence extraction using Ollama-integrated offline models (e.g. Llama 3.1) with no token billing or SaaS recurring subscription costs.
